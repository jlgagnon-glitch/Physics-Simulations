The physics in this run

The track is level and frictionless, so no net external horizontal force acts on the two-cart system during the collision. Total momentum is therefore conserved in every collision type. Kinetic energy is conserved only when the collision is perfectly elastic. The coefficient of restitution e is the ratio of separation speed to approach speed: e = 1 is perfectly elastic and e = 0 means the carts stick together.
